# Escape the Brainrot

> Developed during the **Amazon Games Hackathon 2025**

**Escape the Brainrot** is a fast-paced **2D platformer** built in **Godot Engine**, where the player must break free from the mental chaos of the digital world.  
Your goal: **survive three challenging levels** filled with traps, puzzles, and enemies — and face the **final boss** that embodies “brainrot” itself.

---

## Gameplay

- **Platforming action** across 3 handcrafted levels  
-  **Environmental puzzles** and light combat  
-  **Mini-boss** encounters leading up to a **final showdown**  
-  A surreal theme exploring digital overload and focus recovery  

---

## Built With

- **Engine:** Godot 4.x  
- **Language:** GDScript  
- **Assets:** Custom sprites and tilesets (created during hackathon)  
- **Team:** Pleseanu Cristian, Balalau Andrei, Lican Stefanita (developed in under 48 hours)
